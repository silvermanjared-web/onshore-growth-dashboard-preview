Metric definitions and attribution

Inquiries and stages are synthetic, deduplicated inquiry-cohort counts. SAL means Sales accepted the inquiry for follow-up. SQL means Sales confirmed qualification. Held means an AE meeting occurred. Open sourced pipeline is gross open opportunity value at the stage snapshot. Each opportunity has one primary source. Cash collected is a separate Finance measure by receipt date and may include receipts from earlier or unknown acquisition cohorts.

Paid cost per SAL divides paid-media spend by accepted leads with a paid primary source. All-channel SAL includes paid and nonpaid sources. Partner and event costs use fully loaded allocated cost in the demo. Sourced and influenced opportunity sets can overlap. Assists stay separate from sourced totals. Incrementality requires a separate design.

§179D inquiries do not establish eligibility. Production qualification needs an approved construction-start and eligibility disposition from Sales and Tax. The period selects inquiry cohorts or receipt dates, according to the metric. Stage outcomes use a common January 31, 2027 snapshot, so later cohorts have had less time to mature. Sample checks compare stage balances and receipts with the fictional control totals. Empty denominators display as a dash.

Sales dispositions illustrate how marketing-qualified inquiries split into accepted, rejected and pending decisions. Rejection percentages use only rejected MQLs as the denominator. Age is calendar days in the current stage through the report cutoff. Stage assignments and entry dates are simulated for the same open opportunities counted in the selected cohort.
